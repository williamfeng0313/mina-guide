Twill Weave

nox's bayou

Quick Answer: Twill Weave

Twill Weave is a Defense Trinket in Mina the Hollower. While equipped, it raises Mina's Defense level by one.

Twill Weave is found early in Nox's Bayou, in the Boat Bog area south of the tower.

Twill Weave Details

  • Effect: Raises Defense level by one.
  • Location: Nox's Bayou — Boat Bog.
  • How to Get: Travel south from the tower, climb down the rope, and reach the Trinket on the right.
  • Category: Defense.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.
  • Sell Value: 750 Bones.

How to Get Twill Weave

  1. Enter Nox's Bayou and progress to the tower area.
  2. Head south from the tower into Boat Bog.
  3. Climb down the rope.
  4. Look for the Twill Weave pickup on the right side of the area.
  5. Use a suitable movement option, such as an umbrella Sidearm or Wallower's Gauntlets, to reach the Trinket.

How Does Twill Weave Work?

Twill Weave adds one tier to Mina's effective Defense level while equipped.

Unlike conditional defensive Trinkets, Twill Weave does not require a parry, low health, or a specific damage type. Its benefit is always active while equipped.

Is Twill Weave Worth Using?

Twill Weave is a simple and reliable defensive option. It is especially useful when you want a passive survivability boost without managing a special trigger or timing mechanic.

It can be useful during difficult progression sections or while learning boss patterns, although more specialized Trinkets may offer stronger benefits for specific builds.

Best Uses for Twill Weave

  • Early and mid-game progression.
  • Learning difficult boss patterns.
  • General-purpose defensive builds.
  • Players who prefer an always-active Defense bonus.
  • Cleanup routes where consistent survivability matters more than maximum damage.
