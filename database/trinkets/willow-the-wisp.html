Willow the Wisp

eastern heath

Quick Answer: Willow the Wisp

Willow the Wisp is a combat-focused Trinket in Mina the Hollower. After Mina takes damage, it summons a friendly wisp that can boost her attack damage and attack speed.

Willow the Wisp is obtained from Poppit in the Grassland area of Eastern Heath.

Willow the Wisp Details

  • Effect: After taking damage, summon a friendly wisp that boosts attack damage and attack speed.
  • Location: Eastern Heath — Grassland.
  • How to Get: Obtain Willow the Wisp from Poppit.

How to Get Willow the Wisp

  1. Travel to Eastern Heath.
  2. Progress into the Grassland area.
  3. Find Poppit in the area.
  4. Complete the interaction with Poppit to obtain Willow the Wisp.

How Does Willow the Wisp Work?

Willow the Wisp activates after Mina takes damage. A friendly wisp appears and offers a temporary offensive boost.

When Mina receives the wisp's effect, her attack damage and attack speed increase for a short time. Red particles around Mina indicate that the boost is active.

Current community documentation estimates the attack and attack-speed boost at roughly 25%.

Is Willow the Wisp Worth Using?

Willow the Wisp is useful for players who want an offensive response after taking damage. Instead of only helping Mina survive a mistake, it can turn that hit into a temporary damage and speed opportunity.

Its value is highest in fights where Mina is likely to take occasional damage and can immediately stay aggressive afterward.

Willow the Wisp vs Helio the Wisp

Willow the Wisp and Helio the Wisp both activate after Mina takes damage, but they serve different purposes.

  • Willow the Wisp: focuses on offense by boosting attack damage and attack speed.
  • Helio the Wisp: focuses on recovery by restoring health.

Best Uses for Willow the Wisp

  • Aggressive melee builds.
  • Boss fights where you expect to take occasional damage.
  • Players who want offense instead of healing after being hit.
  • Builds that reward staying aggressive after a mistake.
