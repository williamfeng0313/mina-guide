Wallower's Gauntlets

kindlewood

Quick Answer: Wallower's Gauntlets

Wallower's Gauntlets is a utility Trinket in Mina the Hollower that lets Mina burrow into walls, opening up movement, exploration, and cleanup possibilities beyond normal floor burrowing.

Wallower's Gauntlets is found in Kindlewood along the Wallower's Path route. It is especially useful when revisiting areas, checking hidden paths, or cleaning up missing Trinkets during 100% completion.

Trinket Details

  • Effect: Allows Mina to burrow into walls.
  • Location: Kindlewood — Wallower's Path.
  • How to Get: Follow the Kindlewood map to Wallower's Path and check the relevant chest route.
  • Category: Utility.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.

Is Wallower's Gauntlets Worth Using?

Wallower's Gauntlets is worth using when exploration and cleanup matter more than direct combat power. Being able to burrow into walls can make revisits and hidden-path checks much easier.

It is less important during straightforward boss attempts where damage, defense, or healing Trinkets may offer more immediate value. For 100% completion, however, it is a strong utility option.

Best Uses for Wallower's Gauntlets

  • Exploring walls and routes that normal burrowing cannot handle.
  • Revisiting older areas after movement upgrades.
  • Checking hidden paths during 100% cleanup.
  • Players who prefer utility and route control over raw damage.
  • Pairing with checklist-style cleanup when looking for missing Trinkets.

Wallower's Gauntlets and 100% Cleanup

Wallower's Gauntlets is valuable for cleanup because it changes how you inspect rooms and walls. If you are stuck looking for a missing Trinket, this is the kind of utility item that can make revisit routes easier.

If it is missing from your collection, open the Kindlewood Map & Guide and compare your save against the All Trinket Locations checklist.
