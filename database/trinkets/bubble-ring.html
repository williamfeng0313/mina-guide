Bubble Ring

ossex

Quick Answer: Bubble Ring

Bubble Ring is a Defense Trinket in Mina the Hollower. Strike foes quickly to form a protective shield that absorbs one hit. You can buy Bubble Ring from Lady Susan in Ossex after the Trinket Bazaar expands.

Bubble Ring becomes available after Mina has collected at least 10 Trinkets. It costs 1,500 Bones to purchase.

If you are collecting every Trinket or trying to finish 100% completion, use this page together with the All Trinket Locations, Missable & Easy-to-Miss Trinkets, and 100% Cleanup Checklist.

Bubble Ring Details

  • Effect: Strike foes quickly to form a protective shield that absorbs one hit.
  • Location: Ossex — Trinket Bazaar.
  • How to Get: Purchase Bubble Ring from Lady Susan after the Trinket Bazaar expands.
  • Unlock Requirement: Collect at least 10 Trinkets.
  • Purchase Price: 1,500 Bones.
  • Category: Defense.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.
  • Sell Value: 750 Bones.

How to Get Bubble Ring

  1. Reach Ossex and find Lady Susan's Trinket Bazaar.
  2. Continue collecting Trinkets until Mina has at least 10.
  3. Return to Lady Susan after the Trinket Bazaar expands.
  4. Purchase Bubble Ring for 1,500 Bones.

Is Bubble Ring Worth Using?

Bubble Ring is worth using if you want more forgiveness while learning enemy patterns, exploring dangerous rooms, or revisiting areas during cleanup. It is not always the fastest offensive option, but it can make risky routes feel more consistent.

The shield absorbs one hit. Landing another successful chain of quick attacks refreshes it.

For 100% cleanup, Bubble Ring works best as a safety Trinket. Use it when your goal is to avoid mistakes, protect a run, or safely check old rooms for missed items.

Best Uses for Bubble Ring

  • Safer boss learning when you are still reading patterns.
  • Exploration routes where avoiding chip damage matters.
  • Survival builds for cautious players.
  • Safer backtracking through enemy-heavy rooms during late-game cleanup.
