Glutton's Jug

backwaters

Quick Answer: Glutton's Jug

Glutton's Jug is a quest-stage Trinket in Mina the Hollower. Equip it and defeat 50 enemies to fill the jug.

Glutton's Jug is received from Buffo near the northern entrance of Shanty Swamp in the Backwaters.

After filling the jug, return to Buffo and complete the following encounter to upgrade it into Plasma Jug.

Trinket Details

  • Effect: Equip this jug and defeat 50 enemies to fill it.
  • Location: Backwaters — Received from Buffo near the beginning of the area.
  • How to Get: Speak to Buffo near the northern entrance of Shanty Swamp, where the Backwaters connects with the Western Wilds.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.
  • Upgrade: Becomes Plasma Jug after defeating 50 enemies, returning to Buffo, and completing the following fight.
  • Database Note: Glutton's Jug and Plasma Jug are two stages of the same Trinket slot.

How to Get Glutton's Jug

  1. Travel to the Backwaters.
  2. Reach the northern entrance of Shanty Swamp, near the connection to the Western Wilds.
  3. Find Buffo near the beginning of the area.
  4. Speak to Buffo to receive Glutton's Jug.
  5. Equip Glutton's Jug in one of Mina's Trinket slots.

How to Fill Glutton's Jug

Glutton's Jug only progresses while it is equipped. Defeat 50 enemies with the Trinket active to fill it.

The jug does not provide the finished Plasma Jug combat effect during this stage. Its purpose is to track the required enemy defeats for Buffo's quest.

Enemy-heavy rooms and normal cleanup routes are useful places to build the required count without changing your route too much.

How to Upgrade Glutton's Jug into Plasma Jug

  1. Equip Glutton's Jug.
  2. Defeat 50 enemies while it remains equipped.
  3. Return to Buffo in the Backwaters.
  4. Hand the filled jug back to Buffo.
  5. Buffo becomes hostile and begins a short fight.
  6. Defeat Buffo to upgrade Glutton's Jug into Plasma Jug.

Glutton's Jug vs. Plasma Jug

Glutton's Jug is the temporary quest stage. Its only listed function is to record 50 enemy defeats while equipped.

Plasma Jug is the completed form. It grants bonus Plasma when enemies are defeated and allows Mina's Plasma pool to exceed its normal maximum.

These are not two independent collectible slots. Plasma Jug replaces Glutton's Jug after the upgrade is completed.

Glutton's Jug and 100% Completion

If your inventory still shows Glutton's Jug, the Buffo upgrade quest is not yet complete.

Equip the jug, finish the 50-enemy requirement, and return to Buffo. For the upgraded form and completed effect, see the Plasma Jug guide.
