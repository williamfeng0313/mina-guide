Smelling Salts

Eastern Heath

Effect

Smelling Salts raises Mina's equipped Sidearm level by one, improving the effectiveness of builds that rely heavily on Sidearm damage or utility.

Location

Eastern Heath — found in Frozen Pass, which is accessed through the fast travel train system.

Best Use

Smelling Salts is most valuable in Sidearm-focused builds. Equip it when your Sidearm is a major source of damage, crowd control, or utility rather than an occasional backup tool.

Is Smelling Salts Worth Using?

It is a strong specialist Trinket when your build depends on frequent Sidearm use. Players who rarely use their Sidearm may get more value from a Trinket that improves Mina's main weapon, healing, or defense.
