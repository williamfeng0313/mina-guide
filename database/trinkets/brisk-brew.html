Brisk Brew

ossex

Quick Answer: Brisk Brew

Brisk Brew is a Utility Trinket in Mina the Hollower. It increases Mina's normal walking speed and grants an additional burst of speed for a short time after successfully hitting an enemy.

Brisk Brew is purchased from Lady Susan at the Trinket Bazaar in Ossex.

Trinket Details

  • Effect: Walk faster. Gain a burst of speed for a short time after hitting a foe.
  • Location: Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan at the Trinket Bazaar.
  • Category: Utility.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.
  • Sell Value: 350 Bones.

How Does Brisk Brew Work?

Brisk Brew gives Mina a passive increase to walking speed. After landing a hit on an enemy, she also receives a temporary additional speed boost.

This makes the Trinket useful both during normal exploration and during combat where frequent hits can repeatedly trigger the extra movement speed.

Is Brisk Brew Worth Using?

Brisk Brew is a strong quality-of-life Trinket for players who prefer faster movement. The passive speed increase makes exploration feel quicker, while the post-hit burst can help Mina create distance or reposition during combat.

It does not directly increase damage or defense, so its value depends on how much you benefit from improved mobility and spacing.

Best Uses for Brisk Brew

  • Faster exploration and backtracking.
  • Repositioning after landing attacks.
  • Maintaining spacing against aggressive enemies.
  • Mobility-focused builds.
  • Players who prefer faster movement over direct stat bonuses.

Brisk Brew and 100% Completion

Brisk Brew is obtained from the Trinket Bazaar in Ossex. If it is missing from your collection, return to Lady Susan and check the available shop inventory.
