Evasion Powder

backwaters

Quick Answer: Evasion Powder

Evasion Powder is a defensive Trinket in Mina the Hollower. It keeps Mina invulnerable for longer after taking damage and also grants brief invulnerability after exiting burrow.

Evasion Powder is found in Backwaters. You need to use the movable Ladder to reach the elevated Trinket chest.

Trinket Details

  • Effect: Stay invulnerable longer after damage, and briefly after exiting burrow.
  • Location: Backwaters.
  • How to Get: Use the movable Ladder to reach the elevated Trinket chest.
  • Category: Defense.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.

How Does Evasion Powder Work?

Evasion Powder increases Mina's invulnerability window after taking damage. This gives you more time to move away, recover your position, or avoid being hit repeatedly.

The Trinket also grants brief invulnerability after Mina exits burrow, which gives the effect additional value during aggressive burrow-based movement.

How to Get Evasion Powder

  1. Travel to Backwaters.
  2. Find the movable Ladder in the area.
  3. Bring the Ladder to the elevated platform containing the Trinket chest.
  4. Position the Ladder against the wall and climb up.
  5. Open the chest to collect Evasion Powder.

Is Evasion Powder Worth Using?

Evasion Powder is a strong defensive option for players who struggle with repeated hits. The longer invulnerability window gives you more time to recover after a mistake.

It is especially useful in difficult rooms and boss fights where a single hit can otherwise lead directly into another attack before you have time to reposition.

Best Uses for Evasion Powder

  • Boss fights with fast follow-up attacks.
  • Players who struggle with damage chains after one mistake.
  • Burrow-focused movement and combat.
  • Defensive builds that prioritize recovery windows.
  • First-clear attempts where survivability matters more than maximum damage.

Evasion Powder and Vascular Syrup

Evasion Powder and Vascular Syrup can both support a survival-focused build, but they solve different problems. Evasion Powder extends safety windows after damage, while Vascular Syrup changes how incoming damage is applied over time.

Using both can create a forgiving defensive setup, especially for players still learning difficult enemy or boss attack patterns.
