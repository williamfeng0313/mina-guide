Draining Beastium

kindlewood

Quick Answer: Draining Beastium

Draining Beastium is an Attack Trinket in Mina the Hollower. It increases Sidearm damage, but Sidearms consume more Joules while the Trinket is equipped.

Draining Beastium is found in Kindlewood and can be purchased from the vendor inside the Madd House.

Trinket Details

  • Effect: Sidearms deal more damage but consume more Joules.
  • Location: Kindlewood — Madd House.
  • How to Get: Purchase it from the vendor inside the Madd House.
  • Category: Attack.
  • Slot Cost: 1 Trinket slot.
  • Sell Value: 1,000 Bones.

How Does Draining Beastium Work?

Draining Beastium trades resource efficiency for stronger Sidearm damage. Each Sidearm attack hits harder, but using Sidearms drains Joules more quickly.

The Trinket works best when your build can generate or conserve Joules consistently. Without enough Joule income, the increased cost can make Sidearms harder to use during longer fights.

How to Get Draining Beastium

  1. Progress to Kindlewood.
  2. Reach the Madd House near the farm area.
  3. Complete the Madd House encounter.
  4. Purchase Draining Beastium from the vendor inside the Madd House.

Is Draining Beastium Worth Using?

Draining Beastium is worth considering for Sidearm-focused builds that can support the higher Joule cost. The extra damage can improve burst pressure and make Sidearms more impactful against tougher enemies.

It is less attractive in builds that already struggle with Joule management. In those setups, the higher resource cost may outweigh the damage increase.

Best Uses for Draining Beastium

  • Sidearm-focused damage builds.
  • Boss fights where stronger burst damage is valuable.
  • Builds with reliable Joule generation.
  • Players who use Sidearms selectively rather than constantly.
  • High-damage setups that can tolerate increased resource costs.
