All Trinkets in Mina the Hollower: Locations & Effects

Every trinket in Mina the Hollower — what it does, where to find it, and which detailed guide to read next. You can equip up to 6 at once. Some Trinkets are tied to shops, NPC rewards, hidden routes, bosses, or cleanup conditions.

Quick Answer: All Trinkets in Mina the Hollower

Mina the Hollower has 60 numbered Compendium slots, 61 usable forms including Plasma Jug. Use this database to compare effects, locations, and detailed guide pages for every form. For final cleanup, start with the full list, then open detailed Trinket pages like Bubble Ring, Flame Guard, Watchful Eye, Dead Leaf, Dodging Pendulum, Spring Heels, and Wallower's Gauntlets.

Brisk Brew
Walk faster. Gain a burst of speed for a short time after hitting a foe.
📍 Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan at the Trinket Bazaar.
Seismic Belt
Press Attack while burrowing to emit a damaging pulse around you.
📍 Ossex — Trinket Bazaar shop
Uranium Bracelet
Deal and receive significantly more damage. High risk, high reward.
📍 Ossex — Trinket Bazaar, 2000 Bones (also drops from Nether Kraken)
Intravenous Vial
Automatically use a Vial when Mina has enough Plasma.
📍 Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan after collecting at least 5 Trinkets.
Shock Flint
Attack at full Plasma or full health to fire a deadly shock ball that grows over distance.
📍 Ossex — Trinket Bazaar shop
Bubble Ring
Strike foes quickly to form a protective shield that absorbs one hit.
📍 Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan after the Trinket Bazaar expands.
Counter Vial
Dodge and launch a burst at the attacker if struck while readying a Vial.
📍 Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan after the Trinket Bazaar expands.
Deboning Wand
Enemies reward far more Bones, but the Bones bounce around.
📍 Ossex — Reward from Elizabeth.
Steady Soles
Move normally on water, grass, stairs, and ice. Resist knockback, even into pits.
📍 Ossex — Enter the house right of the fountain via the roof. Burrow under barricade, go through attic.
Valor Medallion
Move slightly while healing. No Plasma lost when struck during a heal.
📍 Ossex — Speak to Furl above the clothes store, hit his nose, bring him flowers, then burrow-boop him.
Bell of Grace
Chance to keep Spark on death. On sparkless death, save half or all Bones.
📍 Ossex — Attack Frayd to remove his Bones, then give him 1,000 Bones at the homeless camp.
Pneumatic Armlet
Supercharge your throws. Thrown objects go faster, further, and deal more damage.
📍 Ossex — Reward from Willis in the Ossex Music Hall.
Warding Beastium
Take less damage, but healing is much less effective.
📍 Ossex — Near Evra in the city.
Thermal Pack
Boosts damage after death or exiting Underlab. Effect fades, then returns stronger.
📍 Ossex — Return to the Miner's Den after saving Fiancé from Bone Beach.
Keri the Wisp
Press and hold Jump while airborne to float and extend your fall.
📍 Southern Outskirts — Reward from Poppit near the Bone Beach Trail.
Primed Vial Pouch
Carry 2 extra Plasma Vials. Using Vials always restores some health.
📍 Southern Outskirts — Small house entered via the roof; chest across a few gaps inside.
Smelling Salts
Raises Sidearm level by one.
📍 Eastern Heath — Frozen Pass, accessed via the fast travel train system.
Willow the Wisp
After taking damage, summon a friendly wisp that boosts attack damage and attack speed.
📍 Eastern Heath — Grassland, obtained from Poppit.
Windfall Charm
Picking up Joules restores some Plasma. Foes hit by Sidearms can drop Joules.
📍 Eastern Heath — Obtained from Lad, the kid flying the green kite.
Chain Capacitor
Attack enemies without missing to boost damage. Missing resets the combo.
📍 Eastern Heath — Complete Wrecker's Choppe Shoppe challenge within 30 seconds.
Twill Weave
Raises Defense level by one.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Boat Bog, south of the tower.
Vascular Syrup
Health decreases very slowly after being hit. You cannot die until the effect runs out.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Near Big Lagoon.
Pit Preserver
Bounce up when falling into a pit or water. Pit damage is reduced.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Buy from the Kindly Hag merchant in her house, 1,000 Bones.
Iron Lung
Increases maximum burrow time, even underwater.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Obtained after defeating Mock Moon.
Tumbling Tutu
Press Jump while airborne to spin attack and bounce off enemies.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Return the small frog to the Bayou Band in Backwaters after beating Nox's Beast.
Volatile Beastium
Defeated enemies sometimes drop a bomb.
📍 Nox's Bayou — Buy from a merchant encountered on the Bone Beach–to–Bayou train route.
Helio the Wisp
After taking damage, summon a friendly wisp that restores your health.
📍 Western Wilds — Purchased from Poppit for 400 Bones.
Flame Guard
Blocks one hit from fire or explosions, then breaks. Recharges in a few seconds.
📍 Western Wilds — Found in a chest inside the Molten Foundry.
Dummy Cache
Press Heal to place a Joule-filled decoy that distracts enemies.
📍 Western Wilds — Bring a snowball from Coltrane Peak's Frozen Pass to Franz.
Dead Leaf
Hold still with this leaf to turn into a silent statue. Breaks if you move.
📍 Western Wilds — Reward from Leaf.
Lace Glove
Raises Attack level by one.
📍 Southern Outskirts / Western Wilds — Ack and Dak encounter sequence.
Spike Spurs
Take less damage and walk steadfastly on spikes without being knocked back.
📍 Knight's Rest — Behind a destructible wall leading to a large spike pit.
Desperation Bonnet
Deal more damage when at low health. Sidearms also cost less at low health.
📍 Mourner's Mile — Old Graveyard, along the Queensbury Crypt route. Burrow beneath the hidden coffin north of the sleeping graveyard keeper.
Proto Spark
Saves Mina from death once with half health. Resets every time you die.
📍 Queensbury Crypt — Complete the Duke questline (find the Duchess).
Stolenoid
Pulls nearby pickups toward Mina with magnetic force.
📍 Queensbury Crypt — Buy from Belvedere's shop in Statue Head Hall, 600 Bones.
Fly Bait
Defeat enemies to attract friendly flies that attack other foes.
📍 Queensbury Crypt — Reward from Midden.
Glutton's Jug
Equip this jug and defeat 50 enemies to fill it.
📍 Backwaters — Received from Buffo near the beginning of the area.
Plasma Jug
Defeating enemies grants bonus Plasma. Plasma can fill above its normal maximum.
📍 Backwaters — Upgrade Glutton's Jug by defeating 50 enemies and returning to Buffo.
Spark Catcher
Grants one extra Spark Container. Hitting foes recovers lost Sparks; loose Sparks fly to you.
📍 Backwaters — Buy from Pinky's Parlor, 500 Bones.
Repulsing Root
When hit, summons a Mourndrake that pushes and damages enemies.
📍 Septemburg — Purchased from Corvidden.
Lightning Grip
Attacking after healing calls down lightning; other hits may also trigger it.
📍 Septemburg / Kindlewood — Campfire Kids reward; later sold by Lady Susan.
Spring Heels
Jump after landing to perform a longer, higher jump.
📍 Radiant Manor — Purchased from a Trooper.
Niter Belt
Creates a radial explosion when emerging from burrow.
📍 Sandfalls — Check during your Trinket cleanup route.
Tunneling Codex
Hold Jump to burrow repeatedly without needing to land.
📍 Sandfalls — Score 5,000 points in the Ring Dive Parlor mini-game.
Joule Syringe
Gain Plasma from Sidearms, unless at 1 HP. At 0 Joules, spend health to use Sidearms.
📍 Bone Beach — Treasure chest.
Bellows Bustle
Press Jump while airborne to perform an air dash.
📍 Complete all three Blaise races in Sandfalls, Ossex, and Western Wilds.
Dodging Pendulum
Press Jump right before taking damage to dodge and become invulnerable.
📍 Radiant Manor — Won from the right Mimic in the two-Mimic room.
Starving Beastium
Using a Vial restores Joules but reduces the Vial's healing.
📍 Queensbury Crypt route — Mina's Grave / hidden zone.
Draining Beastium
Sidearms deal more damage but consume more Joules.
📍 Kindlewood — Madd House. Purchased from the vendor inside.
Reckless Beastium
After healing, your next few hits are powered up, but you're more vulnerable.
📍 Mirror's End — Reward from Dr. Naught.
Burning Beastium
Create damaging fireballs when hit. Jump or burrow to avoid them.
📍 Coltrane Peak.
Watchful Eye
Earn extra Bones for defeating a foe unharmed, as judged worthy by the eye.
📍 Coltrane Peak — Purchased from Rupert.
Blinking Glass
Sometimes, avoid taking damage, and become briefly invulnerable.
📍 Coltrane Peak.
Polyp Lamp
Emits a faint light and reduces damage taken in dark places.
📍 Sandfalls — Purchased from a shop.
Wallower's Gauntlets
Allows Mina to burrow into walls.
📍 Kindlewood — Chest.
Oozing Organ
Leave a damaging trail of ooze behind you whenever you burrow.
📍 Kindlewood — Purchased from the vendor inside the Madd House.
Voltaic Guard
Blocks one hit from lightning attacks, then breaks. Recharges in a few seconds.
📍 Kindlewood — Purchased in the Madd House.
Bridge Weaver
Step off a ledge to summon a web-weaving spider and cross gaps.
📍 Astral Orrery — Found in a chest.
Vial Salvo
Using a Vial launches missiles. Hitting enemies grants bonus Plasma.
📍 Astral Orrery — Purchased from Tubert.
Plasma Funnel
Drink Plasma Vials twice as fast. Don't lose Plasma Vials when hit.
📍 Ossex — Purchased from Lady Susan at the Trinket Bazaar.
Evasion Powder
Stay invulnerable longer after damage, and briefly after exiting burrow.
📍 Backwaters — Use the movable Ladder to reach the elevated Trinket chest.

Trinket Database FAQ

What are Trinkets in Mina the Hollower?

Trinkets are passive items that change Mina's combat, movement, survival, economy, Spark recovery, exploration, or cleanup routing.

Which Trinkets should I check first for 100% completion?

Start with easy-to-overlook reward, shop, boss, and hidden-route Trinkets. Then compare your save against the All Trinket Locations checklist and use the Checklist & Tracker Tool to mark progress.

Are Trinkets only useful for combat?

No. Some Trinkets improve damage, but others help with movement, survival, Bones farming, Spark recovery, exploration, or late-game cleanup.
