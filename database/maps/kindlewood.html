Wallower's Path

Wallower's Path is one of Kindlewood's most important exploration routes. It is directly tied to Wallower's Gauntlets, a utility Trinket used for wall-burrowing and later cleanup routes.

If you are missing this Trinket, use this region map to re-check the Wallower's Path branch, then open the Wallower's Gauntlets guide for the item-specific notes.

Trinkets & Collectibles

MinaHollower currently tracks the following Trinket entries under Kindlewood or its connected route. Use the item pages for exact acquisition notes.

Trinket

Draining Beastium

Sidearms deal more damage but consume more Joules.

Purchased from the vendor inside the Madd House. Trinket

Wallower's Gauntlets

Summon the strength of a Wallower, allowing you to burrow into walls.

Found in a chest. Trinket

Oozing Organ

Leave a damaging trail of ooze behind you whenever you burrow.

Purchased in the Madd House. Trinket

Voltaic Guard

Blocks one hit from lightning attacks, then breaks. Recharges in a few seconds.

Madd House. Trinket

Lightning Grip

Call down a damaging bolt when attacking after healing, and occasionally on hit.

Campfire Kids reward; later available from Lady Susan after the Bazaar expands.
