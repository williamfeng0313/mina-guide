Trinkets & Collectibles

MinaHollower currently tracks the following Trinket entries under Western Wilds or its connected route. Use the item pages for exact acquisition notes.

Trinket

Lace Glove

Raises Attack level by one.

Progress the Ack and Dak encounter sequence involving Bugin. Trinket

Helio the Wisp

After taking damage, summon a friendly wisp that restores your health.

Purchased from Poppit for 400 Bones. Trinket

Flame Guard

Blocks one hit from fire or explosions, then breaks. Recharges in a few seconds.

Found in a chest inside the Molten Foundry. Trinket

Dummy Cache

Press Heal to place a Joule-filled decoy that distracts enemies.

Bring a snowball from Coltrane Peak's Frozen Pass to Franz. Full step-by-step guide → Trinket

Dead Leaf

Hold still with this leaf to turn into a silent statue. Breaks if you move.

Reward from Leaf.
