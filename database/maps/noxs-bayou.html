Trinkets & Collectibles

MinaHollower currently tracks the following Trinket entries under Nox's Bayou or its connected route. Use the item pages for exact acquisition notes.

Trinket

Twill Weave

Raises Defense level by one.

Found in Boat Bog, south of the tower. Climb down the rope and reach the Trinket on the right. Trinket

Vascular Syrup

Health decreases very slowly on hit. You cannot die until it runs out.

Near Big Lagoon. Trinket

Pit Preserver

Bounce up when falling into a pit or water, giving you a chance to recover. Pit damage is reduced.

Purchased from the Kindly Hag. Trinket

Iron Lung

Increases maximum burrow time, even underwater.

Defeat Mock Moon, then collect Iron Lung in the following room. Trinket

Tumbling Tutu

Press Jump while airborne to perform a spin attack and bounce off enemies.

Reward from the Bayou Band.
