Nether Kraken — Mina the Hollower Boss Guide

Tenebrous Sea — opening sequence•Editorial difficulty: 2/5

Quick Answer

Nether Kraken is the opening sea encounter in Mina the Hollower. Current references list it in the Tenebrous Sea, and Mina's story page describes the Kraken attack as the event that sends the ship toward Loner's Landing.

Verified Boss Details

  • Type: Opening boss encounter
  • Verified location: Tenebrous Sea
  • Story role: Attacks Mina’s ship before arrival on Tenebrous Isle
  • Difficulty on this site: 2/5 for a normal first playthrough

First-Clear Strategy

Treat the encounter as an introduction to movement and safe punish timing. The goal is to understand the opening sequence without wasting time on a complicated build plan you do not have access to yet.

Keep your attention on telegraphed threats and return damage only when the arena is stable. New players gain more from surviving consistently than from squeezing in one extra hit.

After the sea sequence, use the walkthrough to continue into the opening regions rather than treating this as a standalone farming or cleanup fight.

Strategy notes are editorial guidance; boss names and locations are checked separately against the current reference list.

Common Mistakes

  • Overcomplicating the opening fight with late-game build advice.
  • Standing in danger to finish a combo when the encounter is teaching basic movement discipline.
  • Searching for the boss later on the island; its verified location is the Tenebrous Sea opening sequence.
