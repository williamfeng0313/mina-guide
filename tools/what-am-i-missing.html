Quick Help

How to read the Skull

What do red eyes mean?

Red eyes usually indicate that the missing collectible is on the current screen. Check breakable walls, burrowable ground, moving platforms, and paths hidden by darkness.

What do blue eyes mean?

Blue eyes usually point toward a nearby connected screen. The connection is not always visually obvious, especially around moving rooms and hidden passages.

Why does Sandy Station flicker yellow or orange?

This unusual clue is strongly associated with Volatile Beastium and the train route. It does not behave like a normal red-or-blue room clue.
