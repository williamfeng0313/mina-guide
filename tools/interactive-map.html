What This Version Tracks

The calibrated beta starts with two regions and eight verified entries. Bone Beach tracks Joule Syringe, Polyp Lamp, and Mined Mind. Sandfalls tracks Niter Belt, Tunneling Codex, Sandy Station, Major Miner, and the route transition into Bone Beach. Sandy Station stays under Sandfalls even when a guide informally calls it the “Bone Beach Train Station.”

Source policy

Verified Baseline Sources

This beta uses the current Mina the Hollower Wiki/Manual as the factual baseline for item, boss, region, and Coltrane Peak classification. GameSpot and a detailed GameFAQs walkthrough are used for the Sandfalls TNT route, Sandy Station access, and the practical transition into Bone Beach. Ambiguous transition screens are treated as boundary notes rather than silently reassigned to a neighboring region.
