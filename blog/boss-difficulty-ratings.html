Boss Difficulty Guide · Updated August 17, 2026

Every Mina the Hollower Boss Rated from 1 to 5 Stars

A consistent first-playthrough difficulty scale using the same verified boss-name and location baseline as our Boss Database and Boss Tier List.

Important: the boss names and locations below are factual reference data; the star ratings are editorial. Route order, weapon choice, Bone Up levels, Sidearms, Trinkets, and player strengths can move any encounter up or down.

Quick Answer: Hardest Bosses

Our current 5-star group is Mined Mind, Frozen Horror, The Congealed, Thalassion, Furgus the Faithful, Radiant Lionel, and Radiant Thorne. For a strict hardest-to-easiest order, use the Boss Tier List.

★★★★★ Very Hard

  • Mined Mind — One of the strongest first-clear execution checks in our editorial ranking. (Bone Beach: Brain Alcove)
  • Frozen Horror — Late-region fight with little room for repeated mistakes. (Coltrane Peak: Icebound Cavern)
  • The Congealed — A late-game wall that asks for consistent movement and resource control. (Astral Orrery: Sealed Archive)
  • Thalassion — An optional encounter best approached after strong preparation. (Backwaters: Fishing Hole)
  • Furgus the Faithful — A demanding late-game encounter in an already punishing stretch. (Radiant Manor: Servants' Quarters)
  • Radiant Lionel — A major endgame confrontation tied to the Prime Generator. (Radiant Manor: Prime Generator)
  • Radiant Thorne — A late-game version of a recurring rival encounter. (Ossex: City Center / Courtyard)

★★★★☆ Hard

  • Nox's Beast — The Bayou's Spark Generator boss and a major movement-pressure fight. (Nox's Bayou: Tainted Lair)
  • The Carving Man — A region-spanning pursuer that culminates in the Carving Shack fight. (Septemburg: Carving Shack)
  • Locomotress Agnes — Movement-heavy pressure in one of the game's late regions. (Coltrane Peak: Maelstrom)
  • Dark Deluxy — An optional encounter linked to the Windy Generator route. (Septemburg: Windy Generator)
  • Baron Lionel — A late-game duel that rewards clean reads and disciplined punish windows. (Radiant Manor: Study)

★★★☆☆ Moderate

  • The Duchess of Queensbury — The Spark Generator boss of Queensbury Crypt and a common first-region boss. (Queensbury Crypt)
  • Mock Moon — A distinct Bayou encounter that becomes more manageable after its rhythm is learned. (Nox's Bayou: Moonlit Path)
  • Madd House — A memorable Kindlewood encounter with a strong central gimmick. (Kindlewood: Overgrowth)
  • Major Miner — A Sandfalls boss encountered in Miner's Den. (Sandfalls: Miner's Den)
  • Lumenarks — A late-region Astral Orrery encounter. (Astral Orrery: Hall of Scholars)
  • Armand — An optional Ossex encounter associated with Legovich's Arms. (Ossex: Legovich's Arms)
  • Midden — A hidden Queensbury Crypt encounter. (Queensbury Crypt: Ancestral Chamber)
  • Mirren — A Coltrane Peak optional encounter. (Coltrane Peak: Frostbite Woods)
  • Wonder Willis — An optional Ossex encounter tied to the Music Hall. (Ossex: Music Hall)
  • Maxi — An optional encounter in Eastern Heath. (Eastern Heath: Grassland)
  • Evra the Undying — An optional fight at Goddred's Grave. (Goddred's Grave)

★★☆☆☆ Easier Once Understood

  • The Dugin — A recurring optional encounter associated with the renegade route. (Ossex / Southern Outskirts / Western Wilds)
  • Thorne — A recurring story boss whose difficulty changes across encounters. (Ossex: Courtyard / Coltrane Peak: Fateful Cliff)
  • Nether Kraken — The opening sea encounter that sets up Mina's arrival on Tenebrous Isle. (Tenebrous Sea)
