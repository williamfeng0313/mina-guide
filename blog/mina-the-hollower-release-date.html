Why does Steam show May 28, 2026?

This is the main reason people can find two different dates when searching for the game. Steam's current store page displays May 28, 2026, while Yacht Club Games' official game page and Nintendo list May 29, 2026.

If you see May 28 on Steam, do not treat it as a second official release date or as evidence that the game was released a day early. It is a storefront/date-display difference. For console availability, the official Nintendo listing uses May 29, 2026, and Yacht Club Games' official game page also uses May 29, 2026.

Important: If you are checking the exact time you can download or launch the game, use the store page for your own region. Storefront unlock times can be displayed differently from the publisher's calendar date.

Was Mina the Hollower delayed?

Yes. The game was originally scheduled for October 31, 2025. In October 2025, Yacht Club Games announced that the team needed additional time for final polish and balancing and delayed the release.

The important point is that the October 2025 date was not the final release date. In February 2026, Yacht Club Games announced that the game would launch in Spring 2026. At that point, the exact day had not yet been locked in because the remaining console submissions were still being completed.

What is Mina the Hollower?

Mina the Hollower is a top-down action-adventure game developed by Yacht Club Games. It combines an 8-bit-inspired visual style with exploration, melee combat, underground burrowing, weapons, Sidearms, Trinkets, character upgrades, bosses, secrets, and New Game Plus.

The game takes place on Tenebrous Isle, where Mina returns to deal with malfunctioning Spark Generators and a growing evil. The main quest involves restoring six failing Spark Generators, with the freedom to approach major areas in different orders.
