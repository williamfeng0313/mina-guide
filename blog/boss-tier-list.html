Mina the Hollower Boss Tier List: Hardest Bosses Ranked

This Mina the Hollower boss tier list ranks major and optional encounters by normal first-clear difficulty: pattern pressure, arena danger, recovery difficulty, preparation needs, and how punishing mistakes feel before you know the fight.

Important: the boss names and locations below are checked against current official/community reference lists, but the difficulty order is editorial. Your weapon, Bone Up level, Sidearm, Trinkets, route order, and playstyle can move a fight up or down a tier.

Quick Answer: What Is the Hardest Boss in Mina the Hollower?

For this site's normal first-playthrough ranking, Mined Mind is the hardest boss overall. It is followed by Frozen Horror, The Congealed, Thalassion, and Furgus the Faithful in the top difficulty tier.

S Tier — Hardest Bosses

The biggest first-playthrough walls. These are the fights most likely to punish weak resource management, greed, or inconsistent movement.

#1 Mined Mind

Location: Bone Beach · Brain Alcove

Our #1 first-clear difficulty pick. The fight creates sustained pressure and gives very little room for repeated mistakes, so clean movement and disciplined damage windows matter more than trying to race the boss.

#2 Frozen Horror

Location: Coltrane Peak · Icebound Cavern

A severe execution check that arrives late enough to expect strong fundamentals. A better build helps, but bad timing is still punished quickly.

#3 The Congealed

Location: Astral Orrery · Sealed Archive

A required late-game wall rather than an optional challenge. The fight asks for movement, pattern recognition, and resource discipline at the same time.

#4 Thalassion

Location: Backwaters · Fishing Hole

An optional encounter that can punish even well-prepared players. Because it is optional, many players reach it with very different builds and progression levels.

#5 Furgus the Faithful

Location: Radiant Manor · Servants' Quarters

A demanding late-game fight inside an already exhausting region. Mistakes are expensive when your focus and healing resources are already under pressure.

A Tier — Very Hard

Serious walls that become more manageable once the core pattern or arena problem is understood.

#6 Nox's Beast

Location: Nox's Bayou · Tainted Lair

A strong early-to-mid-game wall with dangerous arena pressure and enough punishment to expose sloppy movement.

#7 The Carving Man

Location: Septemburg · Carving Shack

Fast pressure and a fight that punishes panic. It gets much easier once you stop forcing attacks and read the timing.

#8 Locomotress Agnes

Location: Coltrane Peak · Maelstrom

Movement-heavy pressure makes careless positioning expensive and can make the fight feel chaotic before the route becomes familiar.

#9 Dark Deluxy

Location: Septemburg · Windy Generator

A difficult optional encounter that rewards preparation. See the Dark Deluxy guide for a dedicated breakdown.

#10 Baron Lionel

Location: Radiant Manor · Study

A fast late-game duel where missed reads can snowball quickly, especially if you enter the sequence with a weak recovery plan.

B Tier — Moderate

Real boss fights that demand attention, but usually become consistent after their central gimmick or rhythm is learned.

The Duchess of Queensbury

Location: Queensbury Crypt

An important early difficulty step that teaches proper dodge-and-punish rhythm.

Mock Moon

Location: Nox's Bayou · Moonlit Path

Tricky at first, but much easier once the encounter rhythm becomes familiar.

Madd House

Location: Kindlewood · Overgrowth

A memorable encounter with enough environmental and execution pressure to punish players who ignore the arena.

Major Miner

Location: Sandfalls · Miner's Den

A solid mid-game test with manageable patterns once you stop overcommitting.

Lumenarks

Location: Astral Orrery · Hall of Scholars

A later encounter that rewards pattern recognition more than raw damage output.

Armand · Midden · Mirren · Wonder Willis · Maxi · Evra the Undying

Optional encounters across Ossex, Queensbury Crypt, Coltrane Peak, Eastern Heath, and Goddred's Grave.

These optional fights can be dangerous when discovered early or unprepared, but usually sit below the game's largest progression walls.

C Tier — Easier Once Understood

Bosses or recurring encounters that are still meaningful, but generally create less first-clear pressure than the tiers above.

The Dugin

Locations include Southern Outskirts and Western Wilds.

A unique optional encounter with an unusual setup, but not one of the largest mechanical walls once you understand what is happening.

Thorne

Locations include Ossex and Coltrane Peak.

Important narratively, but the earlier fight functions more as a combat check than an endgame execution wall.

Nether Kraken

Location: Tenebrous Sea

Visually intimidating, but comparatively manageable after its attacks are understood.

How This Boss Tier List Is Ranked

  • Pattern pressure: how difficult the boss is to read on a first clear.
  • Arena danger: hazards, adds, walls, pits, and movement restrictions.
  • Loadout pressure: how much a weak weapon/Trinket setup increases difficulty.
  • Recovery difficulty: how hard it is to stabilize after taking damage.
  • First-clear punishment: how severely the fight punishes unfamiliarity and greed.

This page ranks distinct major and optional encounters most useful to compare by difficulty. Recurring or transformed versions are not always separated into their own ranking slot.

Recommended Before Hard Bosses

If a boss feels unfair, stop forcing attempts for a moment. A better weapon, safer Trinket setup, more healing capacity, or a different Sidearm can turn a frustrating wall into a learnable fight.

Boss Tier List FAQ

What is the hardest boss in Mina the Hollower?

In this site's normal first-playthrough ranking, Mined Mind takes the #1 spot. The exact answer can change depending on route order, weapon, Bone Up level, Sidearm, Trinkets, and player strengths.

What are the hardest bosses in Mina the Hollower?

Our S Tier contains Mined Mind, Frozen Horror, The Congealed, Thalassion, and Furgus the Faithful. Nox's Beast, The Carving Man, Locomotress Agnes, Dark Deluxy, and Baron Lionel sit immediately below them in A Tier.

Is this an official boss ranking?

No. Yacht Club Games provides official game and boss information, but this difficulty order is an editorial ranking. Boss names and locations are checked against current reference sources; difficulty remains subjective.

Should I change builds for hard bosses?

Yes. If you are dying before learning the pattern, favor survivability and consistency. Once the fight is understood, shift toward damage if faster phases make the encounter easier for you.
